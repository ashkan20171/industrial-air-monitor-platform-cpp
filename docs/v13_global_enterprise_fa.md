## جمله رزومه پیشنهادی
Designed and developed **Ashkan AI Air Monitor**, a C++/Qt industrial environmental monitoring platform with analyzer drivers, Modbus/Serial/TCP communication, OPC-UA SCADA interface, MQTT telemetry, AI anomaly detection, RBAC, audit trail, compliance reporting, Docker/Kubernetes deployment assets, and enterprise documentation.

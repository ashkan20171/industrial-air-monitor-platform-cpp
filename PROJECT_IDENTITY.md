- European industrial automation environments
- SCADA and HMI integration
- Factory air quality monitoring
- Environmental compliance workflows
- IoT and edge-to-cloud telemetry
- AI-assisted anomaly detection
- Industrial cybersecurity-aware deployment
- Portfolio, interview, and enterprise demonstration use

## Short Portfolio Description
Ashkan AI Air Monitor is a modular C++/Qt industrial air-quality monitoring platform created by Ashkan Motaei. It includes analyzer driver abstraction, Modbus/Serial/TCP communication, OPC-UA integration, MQTT telemetry, alarm management, audit trail, RBAC security, AI anomaly detection, reporting, Docker/Kubernetes deployment assets, and compliance-oriented documentation.
